1. No collection, no uploads: there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics SDKs. Everything lives only on your phone.
2. Network use: by default the app only reaches a public code-hosting API to check the version number, sending none of your data. Online speech recognition, the AI assistant and WebDAV backup are opt-in features, all off by default; they connect only after you turn them on and actively use them, sending respectively audio, the sentence you submitted, or your backup file. You can turn any of them off at any time.
3. Due-time reminders and the persistent notification for schedules and anytime tasks are handled entirely on your phone, never via any server.
4. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data; use the export feature first if you want to keep a copy.
5. Questions about this policy? Reach the developer via “Contact the author” in Settings.
