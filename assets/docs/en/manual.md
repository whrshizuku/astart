Start (Chinese name 启序) is a local note-and-task tool: break to-dos into small steps so you can nail just one thing at a time. Designed for ADHD minds — encouraging words, low friction, no scolding. Everything stays on your device by default; apart from update checks, online speech recognition, the AI assistant and cloud backup are all opt-in features that stay off and only connect when you actively use them.

# The rhythm: dump → sort → do
Dump: jot everything down first — no sorting needed.
Sort: when you have a moment, file inbox items into Schedule or Anytime.
Do: keep one focus per day; if it feels big, break it into small steps.

# Home
Top to bottom: focus, big clock, schedule, anytime. Plain text lists you can take in at a glance. A line of encouragement sits beside the clock — finish everything and it reads “All done for today — that’s amazing”.

# Focus
The big headline area at the top. With no focus it shows “One thing at a time”. Tap “Let’s start”: pick an unfinished task from today, or write a new one — you can also long-press any task and drag it to the top to make it the focus. The Focus timer lives on the Focus tab in the bottom bar.
Once chosen, the title shows up big. The main button “Do this one” jumps straight to the steps page to break it down; you can also finish it, pick another, or edit. Completing it shows “Main goal done — beautiful”; tap “Next” to keep going.
Steps: each line you type is saved as a step — press Enter to write several, they split by line on save. The title is just the task name and isn’t split. Finish every step and the task checks itself off.

# Schedule
Timed tasks form a plain text list in time order with the time on the right; overdue items just show the date — no red, no scolding. Today’s phone-calendar events appear alongside as small dots, deduplicated automatically. Future schedules simply show up when their day arrives.
Use the plus button at the top-right to create schedules: write several on separate lines, pick one shared date and time, and they’re all scheduled at once. Saving adds them to your phone calendar and silently sets a system alarm — it reminds you on time, no manual setup.

# Anytime
Everything without a time lives here. The top-right plus lets you write several at once — lines and sentence punctuation split them automatically.
Drag the handle to reorder; tap a row to edit it and give it a time to turn it into a schedule.

# Drag anywhere: into the trash
Long-press any item (schedule, anytime, step, search result) and drag it up — a red zone with a trash can rises from the bottom. Drop it to delete, with a 6-second undo window. In multi-select mode, dragging one selected item deletes the whole group.
Shortcuts: drag a home row to the top to make it the focus; drag an anytime task into the schedule area to give it a time. Every mistake can be undone.

# Search
The magnifier in the bottom bar is the control room for every task: it searches all items (schedules, anytime, steps) by title or note. Filter with the category chips below, tap a result to edit it, or long-press and drag it to the bottom trash to delete.
Use the top-right button for bulk mode: pick items, then drag any one of them to trash the whole group — deletions are undoable.

# Quick capture
The red center button in the bottom bar. Tap it for a quick text note: write first, sort never; tap “Dump in” and it splits by lines and sentence punctuation (。！？；…) into items, then takes you to sorting.
Long-press it for voice capture: by default it uses the phone’s built-in offline engine, no network. It keeps listening after each sentence; tap “Done speaking” to review the text before dumping it in. With the AI assistant enabled, an extra “Tidy with AI” button turns one ramble straight into schedules, anytime tasks and ideas — all undoable as a batch.

# Sort it out
The input bar at the bottom takes quick notes directly: write several on separate lines and they’re split into inbox items on save using the same rules as Quick capture.
Sorting: file each item as a schedule (with a date and time) or an anytime task; it then flows into the matching area on the home page.
In the mind map, tap a node to select it, then add a child or sibling, edit, or delete; drag to move it with its whole subtree, and pinch to zoom.
The import icon copies schedules, anytime tasks and steps into the map for breaking down; long-press a node to edit its text, add a child, or prune the branch.
The top-right plus creates a single inbox item; “Bulk organize” opens bulk actions with select-all.

# Reminders & calendar
When you create a schedule, saving adds it to your phone calendar and silently sets a system alarm — it reminds you on time with no extra steps. You can also toggle the reminder manually in the editor and see the calendar status.
All reminders run on the device and are re-armed automatically after a reboot; the switches live in Settings.

# Focus & stats
Focus: just the ring and a countdown number, nothing to distract you. Pick the duration before you start — presets of 2 / 25 / 45 minutes, or a custom 1–240 minutes — and it’s shown big while running. The per-minute tick, completion cue and volume are adjustable in Settings.
Stats: organized by date — swipe left and right to see focus minutes, sessions, completed tasks, new items and the 24-hour distribution for each day. Plain big numbers, an honest record of progress.

# Data & updates
You can find and delete any item from search, with undo. Settings can export a JSON backup; imports take an automatic snapshot first and are undoable. Uninstalling the app wipes all data, so export first. You’ll be notified when a new version is available.

# Optional extras (all off by default)
Settings → Extras holds three things you can opt into: online speech recognition (lets the system use an online engine for better accuracy), the AI assistant (a free model is preset, or use any OpenAI-compatible endpoint; only the sentence you tap “Tidy with AI” on is ever sent), and cloud backup (WebDAV to your own server or Jianguoyun, manual only). If you don’t turn them on, they never connect; all configuration stays on this device.
